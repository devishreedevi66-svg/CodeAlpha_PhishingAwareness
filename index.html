What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyberattack in which attackers pretend to be trusted individuals or organizations to steal sensitive information such as passwords, OTPs and banking details.

Types of Phishing Attacks

📧 Email Phishing

Fake emails designed to steal personal information.

📱 Smishing

Phishing attacks conducted through SMS messages.

🎯 Spear Phishing

Targeted attacks against specific individuals.

📞 Vishing

Voice-based phishing using phone calls.

🌐 Website Phishing

Fake websites designed to steal login information.

👔 Whaling

Phishing attacks targeting senior executives.

How to Identify Phishing

Suspicious Email

Check the sender's email address carefully.

Urgent Messages

Be careful with messages creating unnecessary urgency.

Unknown Links

Avoid clicking unexpected or suspicious links.

Spelling Errors

Look for unusual grammar and formatting.

OTP Requests

Never share your OTP or password.

Fake Websites

Check website addresses before entering information.

Real-World Phishing Example

📧 Sample Suspicious Email

From: security-alert@example-suspicious.com

Subject: Urgent! Your Account Will Be Suspended

Hello Customer,

Your bank account will be blocked today. Verify your account immediately.

[Suspicious verification link - do not click]

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Unusual sender email address.
  • Creates fear and urgency.
  • Requests immediate verification.
  • Contains an unexpected link.

Social Engineering Techniques

Fear

Threatening users with account suspension.

Urgency

Forcing people to act quickly.

Impersonation

Pretending to be a bank or company employee.

Fake Rewards

Offering fake prizes to attract victims.

Best Practices to Stay Safe

  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Verify the sender before responding.
  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Keep software updated.
  • Use official websites and applications.
  • Report suspicious messages.
